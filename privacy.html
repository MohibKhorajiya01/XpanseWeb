Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 10, 2026

Welcome to Xpanse. Your privacy is our top priority. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we handle your data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard your information within the Xpanse mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data that is strictly necessary for the core functionality of the application:

  • Account Information: When you sign in via Google, we store your Email Address and Display Name. This allows us to securely link your data to your identity, ensuring your financial records are safe and accessible only to you.
  • Financial Records: Any transactions, wallet balances, and budgets that you manually input are stored in our database. This information is used solely to provide you with financial insights and tracking capabilities.
  • Device Metadata: We may analyze basic device information (such as model and software version) to optimize performance and fix technical issues.

2. Data Storage & Security

  • Offline Access: For your convenience, your data is stored locally on your device using a secure Hive Database. This enables you to manage your finances even without an internet connection.
  • Cloud Synchronization: We utilize Google Firebase for secure cloud backups. This process ensures that your data is synced across devices and can be restored if you lose your phone or switch to a new one.

3. Third-Party Integrations

To ensure a high-performance and secure experience, we use trusted infrastructure from Google:

  • Firebase Authentication: For managing secure user logins.
  • Cloud Firestore: To store and sync your financial data securely in the cloud.
  • Google Sign-In: To provide a fast, one-tap authentication method without needing separate passwords.

4. Privacy Guarantees

  • No Data Selling: We never sell, trade, or rent your personal or financial information to third parties.
  • No Intrusive Ads: Xpanse is an ad-free experience. We do not use trackers that monitor your browsing behavior or collect data for advertising purposes.
  • End-to-End Security: All communications between the app and our servers are protected using industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS).

5. User Autonomy & Control

You have full ownership of your data at all times:

  • Modify/Delete: You can edit or delete any transaction entry within the app at any time.
  • Reset Data: You have the option to clear all local data through the application settings.
  • Account Deletion: You can choose to delete your Xpanse account permanently. This action will immediately and irrevocably erase all your data from both your local device and our cloud servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Xpanse is designed for use by individuals aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete it.
